FAQ

Common questions

What does a media buying agency actually do?
A media buying agency plans, launches, and manages your paid advertising campaigns across platforms like Google, Meta, and TikTok — handling strategy, audience targeting, creative testing, budget pacing, and performance reporting, so you don't have to run it in-house.
Which European countries does Nester work with?
We work with brands across the European Union, including Germany, France, the Netherlands, Spain, Italy, Ireland, Belgium, Poland, and Sweden, as well as brands elsewhere in Europe.
How much should I budget for paid media?
It depends on your industry, goals, and competition. During a free audit we review your market and recommend a starting budget and channel mix based on what's realistic for your growth targets.
How is Nester different from a freelance media buyer?
Nester combines strategy, creative testing, and analytics under one accountable process, with transparent weekly reporting tied to revenue rather than vanity metrics — rather than relying on one person's availability.
How long before I see results?
Most accounts see meaningful signal within the first 2-4 weeks of testing, with performance typically stabilizing and improving over 60-90 days as we iterate on data.
Do you require a minimum ad spend or long contracts?
We tailor engagements to each brand's stage and budget — book a free audit and we'll recommend an approach that fits, with no pressure to commit long-term upfront.

Ready to scale your ad spend profitably?

Book a free audit and we'll show you exactly where your budget is leaking — no obligation.
